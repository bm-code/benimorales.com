Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

This policy explains what data Clipper handles. Clipper is a personal tool that runs locally on the user's computer; this website only hosts these documents.

1. This website

This website is static. It has no user accounts, no database, no analytics, no advertising and sets no cookies. Like any web server, the hosting platform may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and requested URL) for operation and security; we do not use them for anything else.

2. How the app processes your data

Clipper runs on the user's own computer. Video files are read, cut, transcribed and rendered locally; they are not uploaded to any server operated by us.

3. TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API

Clipper uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to publish to your own TikTok account. It requests these scopes:

  • user.info.basic: to identify the connected account (for example, display name and avatar) inside the app.
  • video.upload: to send a video to TikTok.
  • video.publish: to publish the video to your account.

4. Access tokens

The access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored encrypted on the user's own computer and are used only to call TikTok on your behalf when you ask the app to. They are not sent to us or to any other third party.

5. Data that leaves your computer

Apart from these, no data leaves your computer.

  • Videos you decide to publish, which are sent to TikTok together with the caption and settings you choose.
  • Optionally, if you turn the feature on: transcript text, sent to an AI provider you configure in order to suggest segments and captions. If you do not enable it, no transcript text is sent.

6. No sale or sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, other than the transfers you initiate described above (TikTok and, if enabled, your chosen AI provider). Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

7. Revoking access

  • In TikTok: Settings → Security & permissions → Apps (or "Manage app permissions"), find Clipper and remove it.
  • In Clipper: disconnect the TikTok account from within the app, which removes the stored tokens from your computer.

8. Your rights

Because your data is stored on your own computer, you control it and can delete it at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data (access, rectification, deletion, objection). For anything related to this website or this policy, contact us and we will respond as reasonably as we can. Data held by TikTok or your AI provider is managed through those services.

9. Children

Clipper is not directed at children, and you must meet TikTok's minimum age requirements to connect an account.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest revision.

11. Contact
